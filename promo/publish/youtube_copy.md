```
This is GoSports — a cartoon two-on-two volleyball game. From the first line of code to the installer on my phone, I built it together with Claude Sonnet 5.5. I only describe things in plain words and say what feels wrong; Claude writes the code, runs the game, takes screenshots and checks them itself.

🏐 How it plays
• Hold a direction to pick your serve target, toss, and hit at the peak — better timing, harder ball
• Bump, set, spike in one flow; hit inside the ring for a "perfect", and combos ignite fever time
• Block at the net, dive to save; instant replays, hawk-eye line calls, press photographers
• Single-player, co-op, versus, tournament and training; keyboard, gamepad and phone touch; Chinese and English

🐟 The gag moments
• Serve too slowly and the animal umpire warns you, then throws pencil stubs, bananas, rubber ducks, slippers, a hammer and the signature sardine. Ignore two warnings and you lose the point
• Make a mistake and your partner steams up, hurls a sardine, or charges over for a punch (the rival team does it too)
• Win a point and it's a random high-five or dance — skip it any time. Lose the match and there might be a falling-out

🛠 How it was made
• Fourteen rounds of conversation. Every round ends with compile checks, translation checks and full-match regression runs, then design docs, encrypted Windows and Android builds, and an adb install onto my phone
• About 21 hours wall-clock from the first message to now (breaks included), 25,000+ lines of code, 33 design-doc sections, 2,600+ tool calls
• Apart from the character pack and the Gemini-made icons, the animation, effects, sound, music and UI are all generated in code
• I also ran several Claude sessions side by side, each on its own branch, building the next game

🏆 The GoSports series: volleyball is first — table tennis, football and shuffleboard are next. Same pick-up-and-play fun, a different sport.
```
